# De-AIGC Checklist

Run this list quickly before returning the rewrite.

## Remove first

- Stock opening frames: "in today's fast-paced world", "it is worth noting that", "overall", "there is no doubt that", or similar generic setups.
- Empty verb-noun bundles: "optimize performance", "enhance efficiency", "conduct analysis", "drive growth", or similar vague verb-object pairs when no precise object is given.
- Triple-stack filler: three similar adjectives or three equivalent claims in a row.
- Generic conclusions that merely restate the paragraph.

## Add or strengthen

- A concrete noun where the sentence currently relies on abstraction.
- A more specific verb where the sentence currently uses a vague operation word.
- Uneven rhythm: mix shorter sentences with longer ones when appropriate.
- Clear emphasis: let one sentence do the real argumentative work instead of making every sentence equally polished.

## Watch for overcorrection

- Do not force colloquial phrases into formal text.
- Do not insert personal voice that the source text did not support.
- Do not replace precise domain terms with casual synonyms.
- Do not delete structure that the genre genuinely needs, such as thesis framing, legal precision, or business action items.
